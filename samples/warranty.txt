Lumen Home - Warranty Terms
(Sample document for the Doc Chat demo. Lumen Home is a fictional store.)

Electronics such as kettles, blenders, irons and lamps come with a one year warranty against manufacturing faults, counted from the delivery date.

Furniture comes with a two year warranty on frames and joints. The warranty does not cover fabric wear, scratches or damage from misuse.

The warranty does not cover damage from power surges, water, drops or repairs done by anyone other than our service partner.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a photo or short video of the problem. If the fault is covered, we repair the item, replace it, or refund it if repair and replacement are not possible.
