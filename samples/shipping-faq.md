# Lumen Home — Shipping FAQ

*Sample document for the Doc Chat demo. Lumen Home is a fictional store.*

## How long does delivery take?

Orders inside Dhaka city arrive the next working day if you order before 3 pm.
Delivery to Chattogram takes 2 to 3 working days.
Delivery to all other districts takes 3 to 5 working days.

## How much does delivery cost?

Delivery inside Dhaka costs 60 taka. Delivery outside Dhaka costs 120 taka.
Orders above 3,000 taka ship free anywhere in Bangladesh.

## Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available everywhere we ship. You can also pay online by card or mobile wallet when you place the order.

## How do I track my order?

You get an SMS with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse. You can also find the tracking link under My Orders in your account.

## Can I change my delivery address?

You can change the address until the order is packed. Contact support with your order number and the new address. Once the parcel has left the warehouse, the address can no longer be changed.

## Do you ship outside Bangladesh?

No. At the moment we only deliver inside Bangladesh.
